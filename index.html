About us

May and Jerrold met at NUS. Though they came from vastly different faculties, they found each other in an unexpected place: while volunteering.

They soon realised they were like pieces from two different jigsaw puzzles that somehow fit together. When everyone else thought Jerrold’s sense of humour was cold, May thought it was so funny.

With that, they began their journey of navigating life together, from their youth into adulthood, and on into the future.
